About

Angularity FW is a hobby project: a heavily modded Flipper Zero firmware, shared in the hope it’s useful.

Not an official product

Angularity FW is unofficial. It is not made, endorsed, sponsored or supported by Flipper Devices Inc. or by the Momentum Firmware team, and nobody from those projects can help with problems in this firmware. “Flipper” and “Flipper Zero” are trademarks of their respective owners; they are used on this site only to name the hardware the firmware runs on.

Credits

  • Momentum Firmware by Next-Flip and contributors — the base this is built on (itself derived from the official Flipper Zero firmware). Angularity is based on Momentum MNTM-012.
  • Flipper Devices Inc. — the Flipper Zero, its firmware and the mobile apps that qAngularity forks (MIT licensed).
  • jblanked — FlipperHTTP, the WiFi Devboard firmware and companion apps this project’s network features rely on.
  • Open-Meteo and timeapi.io — the free weather and time services used by Standby.
  • All the authors of the third-party apps bundled in the Momentum app catalog.

  • Fonts and artwork: third-party components keep their own licenses; the font review is in the qAngularity docs/LICENSES.md.

Responsible use

Radio, RFID/NFC, infrared and USB features are regulated in many places. Use this firmware lawfully, only on devices, systems and signals you own or have permission to test. You flash and use it at your own risk; it comes with no warranty.

Privacy of this website

This is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and has no analytics or ads. The Downloads page and the home page’s “latest release” badge ask api.github.com and raw.githubusercontent.com for release information from your browser, so GitHub sees those requests as it would for any visit to its sites — see GitHub’s privacy statement.
